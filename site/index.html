CNCF Agent Substrate: many stateful agents, few pods

Agents spend most of their life waiting. Substrate snapshots idle actors out of memory and resumes them on any warm worker when a request arrives, each in gVisor, with egress and credentials kept outside. This lab measures whether that holds on your own cluster.
